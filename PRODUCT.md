# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, graduate students, and academic writers who read English scientific papers, conduct literature reviews, compare evidence across studies, or revise scientific prose. They may work with unpublished manuscripts and need to inspect where each AI-assisted observation came from.

## Product Purpose

PaperLab helps people understand research papers and improve scientific writing. It supports paper import and reading, evidence-linked analysis and extraction, cross-paper comparison, and paragraph evaluation, generation, and revision. Its success is measured by clear source traceability, useful researcher-controlled workflows, and writing changes that preserve the supplied evidence and claim strength.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clear, and trustworthy. The product should feel calm and precise while keeping researchers in control of AI suggestions and decisions.

## Anti-references

- Avoid dense decoration, gratuitous gradients, and visual effects that compete with papers and evidence.
- Avoid presenting model output as verified fact or hiding its source and uncertainty.
- Avoid oversized marketing-style hero sections inside task-oriented screens.

## Design Principles

1. Put the research task and its next action first.
2. Keep findings connected to their source passage and location.
3. Separate extracted observations, AI interpretations, and researcher decisions.
4. Keep original paper text immutable; make revisions reviewable and reversible.
5. Use concise, consistent interface patterns that help researchers work without visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, and semantic controls. Never use color as the only status indicator. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Use Vietnamese interface copy and preserve English source text exactly.
