## Observed on a real 32-page two-column ICML paper (acceptance target)

Claude runs a local probe on the real PDF after this work (not committed). Current behaviour and required outcome:

| #   | Today                                                                                                                            | Required                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Wrapped headings keep only the first line ("Current landscape of AI reviewing tools and")                                        | Join the continuation line when it is in the same column, starts at the heading's x, is short, is not a sentence and has no citation parenthesis                                          |
| 2   | A right-column subsection heading is lost ("Results: Hivemind effect in simulation")                                             | Headings in either column are detected                                                                                                                                                    |
| 3   | Last right-column sections come out of order (Acknowledgements, Rethinking…, Conclusions)                                        | Reading order per page: full-width blocks in vertical position, then the left column top to bottom, then the right column                                                                 |
| 4   | Superscripts split off ("rd" of "43rd", footnote marks)                                                                          | Merge raised, smaller, adjacent fragments into their line                                                                                                                                 |
| 5   | Paragraphs cut at column or page breaks ("… be triv-" / next part elsewhere)                                                     | A paragraph continues across a column or page break when the previous line has no terminal punctuation and the next starts in lowercase or continues a hyphenated word                    |
| 6   | Each reference line is its own paragraph, and body lines are interleaved on reference pages                                      | One paragraph per reference entry: a new entry starts at a line with the entry's hanging-indent x or a `Surname, X.` / `[n]` start; continuation lines join it; body text is not mixed in |
| 7   | After References, appendix prompt/code lines become headings ("PAPER TITLE: {title}", "OUTPUT FORMAT:"), about 30 false sections | Reject headings containing `{`/`}` or ending with `:` in all caps; lines in a monospace or boxed block are not headings                                                                   |
| 8   | Sections after References get role `other`                                                                                       | Real headings after the reference list (e.g. "A Limitations", "B.1 …") get role `appendix` and subsections inherit it                                                                     |

Column detection must be per page: cluster line start x positions; a page is two-column only when both clusters hold
a meaningful share of lines and a central gutter is empty over most of the page height. Single-column papers must behave
as before. Page-level full-width elements (title block, wide figures/tables, wide captions) are lines whose x-range crosses
the gutter.
